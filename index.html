What we make

Starter range: four crops, one processing line

Amla

Powder for vitamin C drinks, health and Ayurvedic products. Sourced locally from Pratapgarh, home of GI-tagged amla.

Beetroot

Spray-dried juice powder for natural colour, sports nutrition and beverage blends.

Chia

Chia oil and chia protein flour: value-added ingredients made in India instead of exporting raw seed.

Moringa

Leaf powder for nutrition and export markets. India is the world’s largest moringa producer.

The plant

A phased build in Uttar Pradesh

Phase 1 · Now

250 kg/h

A complete multi-product line: extraction, evaporation, spray drying, utilities and a by-product line, on our own plot and building on a UPSIDA industrial estate.

Phase 2 · Scale-up

1,000 kg/h

A second, larger line on land reserved in phase 1, once products and buyers are proven.

Capacity is the water removed per hour by the evaporator and spray dryer.
