About Blue Panthers India

Blue Panthers Manufacturing Private Limited was founded in Uttar Pradesh in 2026. We are building a multi-product plant that turns Indian crops into export-grade functional ingredients, and we share the expertise behind it through food process and regulatory consulting.

Our purpose

Add the value in India

India grows a vast range of crops, yet only a small share is processed here, and Indian food companies often struggle to meet the technical evidence that regulators and export buyers expect.

We are building a plant that processes Indian crops in India, close to the farms, to the standards global buyers expect, using food-process expertise developed through research in Canada.

What we do

Manufacturing first, backed by consulting

Manufacturing

Functional ingredients

Our core business: a multi-product plant in Uttar Pradesh making amla, beetroot, chia and moringa ingredients for Indian and export buyers.

Our products Consulting

Food process & regulatory consulting

Process Authority review, FSSAI and US FDA compliance, food-safety validation, labelling and product development for other Indian food companies.

Consulting services

Our values

A sustainable, honest, enlightened enterprise

S

Sustainable

Zero-waste processing, water-based extraction and energy-efficient drying. Every part of the crop is sold as product or by-product.

H

Honest

Advice based on evidence. We issue letters and conclusions only when the process and data support them.

E

Enlightened

Research-led processing that adds value in India and creates rural jobs at the plant and in the supply chain.
