About Blue Panthers India

Blue Panthers Manufacturing Private Limited was founded in Uttar Pradesh in 2026. We provide food process and regulatory consulting to Indian food companies, and we are building our own functional-ingredients plant.

Our purpose

Add the value in India

India grows a vast range of crops, yet only a small share is processed here, and Indian food companies often struggle to meet the technical evidence that regulators and export buyers expect.

We bring research-grade food-process expertise, the same that supports processors in Canada and the United States, to Indian manufacturers, and apply it in our own plant.

What we do

Two businesses, one body of expertise

Consulting

Food process & regulatory consulting

Process Authority review, FSSAI and US FDA compliance, food-safety validation, labelling and product development for Indian processors.

Our services Manufacturing

Functional ingredients

A multi-product plant in Uttar Pradesh making amla, beetroot, chia and moringa ingredients for Indian and export buyers.

Our values

A sustainable, honest, enlightened enterprise

S

Sustainable

Zero-waste processing, water-based extraction and energy-efficient drying. Every part of the crop is sold as product or by-product.

H

Honest

Advice based on evidence. We issue letters and conclusions only when the process and data support them.

E

Enlightened

Research-led processing that adds value in India and creates rural jobs at the plant and in the supply chain.
