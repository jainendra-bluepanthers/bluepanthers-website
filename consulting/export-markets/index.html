Export markets

Exporting adds a second set of rules on top of FSSAI: Indian export bodies on one side, and the importing country's food law and buyer standards on the other. We help you prepare for both.

From India

Indian export requirements

Depending on the product and destination, exporters may need registration with an export promotion body, pre-shipment inspection or health certification. We help assemble the technical evidence behind these applications.

  • Mapping your product to APEDA, Spices Board or other export bodies
  • Export Inspection Council (EIC) certification readiness, where required
  • Specifications and certificates of analysis for buyers
  • Testing plans for pesticide residues, contaminants and microbiology
  • Shelf-life and packaging evidence for long transit times
  • Supplier and raw-material traceability
Export requirements change by product and destination, and some are set by the buyer rather than the regulator. We confirm the applicable requirements for your product and market before proposing work.
