Engagement & fees

Scope and expected hours are confirmed in writing before technical work begins. Requirements vary by product, process, evidence and market.

Start your review Review checklist

Engagement process

From enquiry to deliverable

  1. 01

    Consultation request

    Describe the product, process, market and the outcome you need.

  2. 02

    Fit and conflict check

    We confirm the work is within our scope and identify immediate requirements.

  3. 03

    Scope and fee quote

    You receive written engagement terms, expected hours, fees in INR and payment instructions.

  4. 04

    Technical review

    We review the documents and identify questions, gaps or validation needs.

  5. 05

    Revisions or testing

    You make the required changes and authorise any additional work.

  6. 06

    Deliverable

    We provide the agreed report, filing support or letter when technically warranted.

Fees

Clear, written terms

Fees are quoted in Indian rupees, plus GST, after we understand the scope. Most reviews begin with an initial retainer that covers document review, consultation, recommendations and preparation of the deliverable.

Export clients may be billed in US dollars.

  • Fixed-scope quotes for defined deliverables
  • Hourly retainer for broader or ongoing work
  • Support for MSMEs and early-stage food businesses
  • Laboratory testing, travel and third-party services quoted separately
  • No work beyond the agreed scope without your written approval
What a retainer does and does not buy. Scope acceptance and retainer receipt allow technical work to begin. They do not purchase or guarantee a letter, a filing outcome, regulatory acceptance or product approval.
