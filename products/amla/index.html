Amla powder

Indian gooseberry, one of the richest natural sources of vitamin C, processed into a stable powder close to where it grows: Pratapgarh in Uttar Pradesh, home of GI-tagged amla.

Request specification & sample All products

Applications

Where amla powder is used

Amla has a long history in Indian food and Ayurveda, and growing demand worldwide as a natural vitamin C ingredient.

  • Vitamin C and immunity drinks
  • Health supplements and nutraceuticals
  • Ayurvedic formulations
  • Juice and beverage premixes
  • Health foods and snacks
  • Confectionery and candies
Source

Grown next to the plant

Fresh amla from Pratapgarh, bought in season directly from farmers and farmer producer organisations, close to our plant.

Process

Gentle, water-based

Cleaned and graded fruit is processed on our water-based extraction, evaporation and spray-drying line, with no harsh solvents.

Supply

Available now

Supplied today through our white-label range, made by Indian partners, and from our own plant once phase 1 is running.

Specification

Available on request

Our specification sheet and certificate of analysis cover the parameters below. Tell us your application and we will share the sheet that fits it.

  • Appearance, colour and taste
  • Moisture and water activity
  • Particle size and solubility
  • Vitamin C content
  • Microbiological profile
  • Heavy metals and pesticide residues
