Review checklist

Prepare these items before your review begins. A complete package shortens review time and reduces follow-up questions.

Every review

Core documents

Needed for every product, whatever the process or market.

  1. Product description: name, category, intended use and consumer, and target markets.
  2. Complete formulation: every ingredient with percentage, supplier and specification sheet.
  3. Process flow diagram: each step with times, temperatures, pressures and hold points.
  4. Batch size and equipment: kettles, retorts, pasteurisers, fillers, dryers and their capacities.
  5. Packaging: container type, size and material; closure or seal; fill weight and headspace.
  6. Shelf life and storage: target shelf life, storage temperature and distribution conditions.
  7. Existing test results: pH, water activity, Brix, moisture, salt and microbiology, with lab names.
  8. Licences: FSSAI licence number and category; FDA registration numbers if exporting to the US.
  9. Current controls: HACCP/FSMS plan, monitoring records and deviation procedures, if any.

Product-specific

Additional evidence by process

Acidified foods

Pickles, sauces, chutneys

  • Equilibrium pH of finished product (target and maximum)
  • Acidification method and acid used
  • Particle size and pH equilibration time
  • Hot-fill or pasteurisation temperature and hold time
  • pH measurement method and calibration records
Low-acid canned foods

Retort pouches, cans, jars

  • Retort type, venting schedule and come-up time
  • Heat-distribution study for the retort
  • Heat-penetration data for the product and container
  • Initial temperature, fill weight and headspace limits
  • Seal or seam inspection procedure and records
Juices & beverages

Juices, pulps and drinks

  • Pasteurisation or HTST time and temperature
  • Pathogen reduction target and supporting data
  • Brix, acidity and pH specifications
  • Filling temperature and container sterilisation
Dried & powdered

Powders, spices, dehydrated foods

  • Water activity and moisture of finished product
  • Drying conditions: inlet and outlet temperatures, residence time
  • Microbial reduction step (steam, heat), if any
  • Packaging moisture barrier and storage humidity
