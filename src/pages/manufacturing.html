Value-added functional ingredients from Indian crops

We are building a multi-product processing plant in Uttar Pradesh that turns Indian crops into stable, export-grade powders and oils for food, beverage and nutrition brands.

What we make

Starter range: four crops, one processing line

Amla

Powder for vitamin C drinks, health and Ayurvedic products, sourced from Pratapgarh, home of GI-tagged amla.

Beetroot

Spray-dried juice powder for natural colour, sports nutrition and beverage blends.

Chia

Chia oil and chia protein flour, made in India instead of exporting raw seed.

Moringa

Leaf powder for nutrition and export markets. India is the world's largest moringa producer.

Who we supply: bakery, beverages, health foods, nutrition and sports brands, and nutraceutical makers in India and abroad. Side streams from every crop are sold as feed or food inputs.

The plant

A phased build in Uttar Pradesh

Phase 1 · Now

250 kg/h

A complete multi-product line: extraction, evaporation, spray drying, utilities and a by-product line, on our own plot and building on a UPSIDA industrial estate.

Phase 2 · Scale-up

1,000 kg/h

A second, larger line on land reserved in phase 1, once products and buyers are proven.

Capacity is the water removed per hour by the evaporator and spray dryer.
