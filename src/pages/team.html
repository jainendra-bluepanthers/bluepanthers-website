AP

Food engineering · Bioproducts · Process Authority

Prof. Anubhav Pratap-Singh

Co-founder · Process development

Associate Professor of Food Processing at the University of British Columbia, where he leads the Food Process Engineering Laboratory. Co-founder of Sustainable Bioproduct Innovations and of Blue Panthers Technology Inc., Vancouver, where he serves as Process Authority. He leads process development for Blue Panthers India.

His work spans process development, optimisation, prototype development and scale-up, with platforms including thermal processing, extrusion, HTST/UHT, high-pressure processing, microwave-vacuum dehydration and other non-thermal technologies.

Research areas

  • Advanced food processing and preservation
  • Sustainable food ingredients and bioproducts
  • Thermal and non-thermal processing
  • Process development, validation and scale-up

Education

Banting Postdoctoral Fellow, University of Toronto; PhD in Food Science, McGill University; BTech/MTech in Chemical Engineering, IIT Kharagpur.

AS

Food chemistry · Natural health products · Applied research

Dr. Anika Singh

Food chemistry & applied research

Food and bioproduct researcher and innovation leader. She leads applied research at the interface of post-harvest processing and food chemistry, helping food companies solve research and development challenges, and is co-founder of Blue Panthers Technology Inc.

Her work includes food-component analysis, bioactive delivery, emulsions and nanocarriers, packaging films, novel processing technologies, food safety and microbial validation.

Research areas

  • Post-harvest processing
  • Food chemistry and HPLC/GC analysis
  • Bioaccessibility and bioavailability
  • Food quality, safety and process validation

Education

PhD in Food Science, McGill University; MTech in Post-Harvest Processing and Food Engineering; BTech in Agricultural Engineering.
