India · FSSAI

We help Indian processors turn the requirements of the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India and State Food Safety Departments into practical technical documents and controls.

Licensing & product standards

The Food Safety and Standards Act and its regulations

Every food business in India operates under an FSSAI registration or licence. The right licence category, the product standard that applies, and whether an ingredient needs prior approval all shape what you can make, how you label it and where you can sell it.

  • Licence category and scope review
  • Food-category and product-standard mapping
  • Additive and processing-aid permissibility checks
  • Non-specified food and ingredient approval support
  • Health supplement and nutraceutical categorisation
  • Technical documents for licence modification

Food safety in the plant

Hygiene, FSMS and inspection readiness

Licensed manufacturers must follow the hygiene and sanitary practices in Schedule 4 and operate a Food Safety Management System. Inspectors, third-party auditors and buyers will ask for the evidence behind it.

  • FSMS and HACCP plan development
  • Schedule 4 GHP/GMP gap assessment
  • Process flow and hazard-control documentation
  • pH, water activity and shelf-life evidence plans
  • Thermal-process and cooling validation
  • Testing plans with NABL-accredited laboratories
  • Traceability and recall procedures
  • Responses to inspection and audit observations
Domestic and export rules differ. A product sold only in India, the same product exported to the United States and an ingredient sold to another manufacturer may follow different regulatory pathways. We identify the applicable scope before proposing work.
