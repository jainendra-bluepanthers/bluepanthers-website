Food safety & validation

A food-safety system is strongest when its controls are tied directly to the product, process, equipment and distribution conditions.

Services

Turn technical conclusions into practical production records

We help processors identify the evidence needed to support each control, and build systems that operators on the shop floor can use consistently.

  • Process-flow and hazard-control review
  • Critical-factor identification
  • HACCP and FSSAI FSMS plan development
  • Scheduled-process and thermal-process support
  • Heat-distribution and heat-penetration study planning
  • Cooling-process assessment
  • Microbiological and shelf-life study planning
  • Environmental and finished-product monitoring
  • Batch-record and deviation-management templates
  • Corrective-action and revalidation planning
  • Gap assessment against FSSAI Schedule 4
  • Readiness for BRCGS, FSSC 22000 and buyer audits

Standards we work to

One system for domestic and export requirements

India

FSSAI hygiene & FSMS

Good hygiene and manufacturing practices under Schedule 4 of the FSSAI licensing regulations, Food Safety Management System plans and third-party audit readiness.

United States

FSMA preventive controls

Hazard analysis and risk-based preventive controls for human food (21 CFR 117), and food-safety plans prepared for review by your PCQI and your US importer's FSVP.

Global

GFSI certification

Process and validation evidence that supports BRCGS, FSSC 22000 and retailer or brand-owner audits.

Validation may require NABL-accredited laboratories, pilot facilities or instrumentation providers. Blue Panthers helps define the study and interpret the evidence. Third-party services are separately authorised and billed.
