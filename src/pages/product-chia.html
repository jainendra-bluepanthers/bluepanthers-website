Chia oil & chia protein flour

India exports most of its chia as raw seed. We turn the seed into two higher-value ingredients in India: an oil and a protein-rich flour.

Register your interest All products
Ingredient 1

Chia oil

Oil pressed from chia seed, a plant source of omega-3 fatty acids.

  • Nutraceuticals and supplements
  • Functional foods and beverages
  • Dressings and cold applications
Ingredient 2

Chia protein flour

The seed that remains after pressing, milled into a flour rich in protein and fibre.

  • Protein bars and snacks
  • Bakery and gluten-free products
  • Plant-based foods and blends
Source

Indian chia seed

Seed bought from Indian growers and mandis, such as Neemuch in Madhya Pradesh.

Process

One seed, two products

Pressing separates the oil; the remaining press cake becomes protein flour, so the whole seed is used.

Supply

From our own plant
