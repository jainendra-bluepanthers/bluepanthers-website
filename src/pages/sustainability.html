Built to be a sustainable, honest, enlightened enterprise

Adding value in India means more of each crop is used, more of its value stays with farmers and rural communities, and less is lost after harvest.

S

Sustainable

Zero-waste processing, water-based extraction and energy-efficient evaporation. Every part of the crop is sold as product or by-product.

H

Honest

Traceable batches, clear specifications and direct and FPO sourcing that link farmers to the value their crops create.

E

Enlightened

Research-led processing that adds value in India and creates rural jobs at the plant and in the supply chain.

Zero waste

The whole crop, not just the best part

Our separation step splits each crop into the main product and side streams. A by-product line, built in phase 1, turns those side streams into saleable feed and food inputs. With chia, pressing gives an oil and the remaining seed becomes protein flour.

Processing near the farm also reduces the post-harvest losses that cost India an estimated ₹1.5 lakh crore of produce every year.

Jobs

Value

Value added in India
