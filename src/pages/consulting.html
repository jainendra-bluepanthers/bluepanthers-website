Regulatory scope

Indian and U.S. regulatory pathways

Indian and U.S. food rules share the same food-safety purpose but differ in regulators, terminology, submissions and evidence. We map the work to the right jurisdiction before it starts.

India · FSSAI

Indian national pathway

Support for processors working with the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India, State Food Safety Departments, export bodies and buyer audits.

  • FSSAI licence scope and category review
  • Food Safety Management System (FSMS) and HACCP plans
  • Product-standard, additive and approval checks
  • Labelling and claims review
  • Responses to inspections, audits and buyer questionnaires

United States · FDA

U.S. FDA pathway

Support for Indian processors exporting foods to the United States, including acidified foods and low-acid canned foods in jars, cans and retort pouches.

  • Product and regulatory classification
  • Food facility and FCE registration support
  • Forms FDA 2541d, 2541e, 2541f and 2541g
  • SID review, corrections and supplements
  • Import refusal and FDA correspondence response

Engagement

Scope and review process

We first identify the product category, jurisdiction, available evidence and the deliverable you need. Work then proceeds under a written engagement with an agreed scope; laboratory testing and validation studies are scoped separately.

Engagement and fees
  1. Enquiry: describe the product, process, market and the outcome you need.
  2. Fit and conflict check: we confirm the work is within scope.
  3. Scope and engagement letter: expected hours, deliverables and payment terms in writing.
  4. Technical review: we review your documents and identify gaps.
  5. Deliverable: report, filing support or letter when technically warranted.
