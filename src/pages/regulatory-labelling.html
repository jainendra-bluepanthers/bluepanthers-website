Regulatory & labelling support

A formulation change can affect safety, claims, ingredient declarations, nutrition information, processing behaviour and shelf life. We help you address these decisions together.

Scope

Navigate evidence, claims and technical documentation

  • Regulatory pathway and evidence-gap assessment
  • Ingredient, additive and food-category classification
  • Product-standard checks under FSSAI regulations
  • Non-specified food and novel ingredient approval support
  • Health supplement and nutraceutical category review
  • Label and ingredient-list review for India and the US
  • Nutrition information and claim-support planning
  • Responses to regulator, auditor or customer questions
  • Coordination with NABL-accredited laboratories

Labels

One product, two labels

An Indian label and a US label for the same product follow different rules. We check both against the same formulation.

India

FSSAI labelling

  • Mandatory declarations under the Labelling and Display Regulations, 2020
  • Veg / non-veg symbol, FSSAI logo and licence number
  • Nutrition information and serving size
  • Allergen and additive declarations
  • Claims under the Advertising and Claims Regulations, 2018
United States

FDA labelling

  • Statement of identity and net quantity
  • Ingredient statement in descending order
  • Nutrition Facts panel format
  • Major food allergen declarations
  • Nutrient content and health claim review
Blue Panthers provides technical consulting and application support. Legal opinions and final regulatory decisions remain with the applicable regulator or qualified legal counsel.
