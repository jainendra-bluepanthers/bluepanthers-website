Functional food ingredients · Uttar Pradesh, India

Value-added functional ingredients from Indian crops

We are building a multi-product processing plant in Uttar Pradesh that turns amla, beetroot, chia and moringa into stable, export-grade powders and oils for food, beverage and nutrition brands in India and abroad.

The opportunity

India grows the crops. The value is added elsewhere.

India is one of the world's largest producers of fruit, vegetables and seeds, yet most of it leaves the farm unprocessed. We process Indian crops in India, close to where they are grown.

16–17%Share of India's farm produce that is processed today. The national target is 25–30%.
₹1.5 lakh CrFarm produce lost every year for want of storage and processing.
US$8.9 bnIndia's nutraceuticals market in 2025, growing about 11% a year to 2034.

Sources: IBEF (Sep 2026) for processing level and post-harvest losses; IMARC Group, India Nutraceuticals Market (2025 base).

Plant & process

The plant

A phased build in Uttar Pradesh

Phase 1 is a complete multi-product line, with extraction, evaporation, spray drying, utilities and a by-product line, on our own plot on a UPSIDA industrial estate. Phase 2 adds a larger line on land reserved in phase 1, once products and buyers are proven.
