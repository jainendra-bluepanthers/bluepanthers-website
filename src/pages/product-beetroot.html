Beetroot powder

Spray-dried beetroot juice powder: a natural source of deep red colour for food and drink, and a popular ingredient in sports and beverage blends.

Applications

Where beetroot powder is used

Food brands are replacing synthetic colours with plant-based ones, and beetroot gives a strong red to pink shade that consumers recognise.

  • Natural colour for bakery and confectionery
  • Dairy, frozen desserts and yoghurts
  • Sports and pre-workout blends
  • Beverages and smoothie mixes
  • Soups, sauces and seasonings
  • Health foods and supplements
Source

Bought at harvest-season lows

Fresh beetroot from Indian farms in Uttar Pradesh and neighbouring states, bought when supply is highest.

Process

Juice, concentrate, spray dry

Beetroot is juiced, concentrated by energy-efficient evaporation and spray dried into a stable, free-flowing powder.
