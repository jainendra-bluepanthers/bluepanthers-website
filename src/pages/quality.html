Quality & food safety

Food safety is designed into the plant from the first drawing, not added at the end. Our aim is that every batch can go to a buyer in India or abroad.

Our approach

Built to export-grade expectations

01

Hygienic design

Food-grade stainless-steel contact surfaces, hygienic layouts and separated zones for raw material and finished powder.

02

Validated process

Critical process steps identified and validated with the same methods our consulting team uses for clients.

03

Tested batches

In-house quality-control laboratory for routine checks, with NABL-accredited laboratories for full analysis.

04

Traceability

Each batch traced back to its raw-material lot and supplier, and forward to the customer.

05

Supplier control

Raw-material specifications and approved suppliers, from farmers and FPOs to packaging.

06

Documentation

Specification sheets, certificates of analysis and food-safety documents ready for buyer audits.

Standards

The standards we are designing to

The plant is being designed so that it can be certified to these standards. Certification takes place once production starts; we will list each certificate here as it is granted.

  • FSSAI licence and Schedule 4 hygiene requirements
  • HACCP-based food-safety management system
  • BRCGS Global Standard for Food Safety
  • US FDA food facility registration and preventive controls
Where we are today. The plant is under development and certifications are not yet held. Our white-label products are made by Indian partner manufacturers; ask us for the certifications that apply to each product.
